## Phase 3 · One host confirms the booking

`1dd5f33` implements the final host decision, followed by the reviewed replay fix at `2174d7b`. A host sees the exact proposal, affirms the household will be home and confirms or declines. The web service checks membership, serializes against household booking writes, locks the submitted version and rechecks inventory, exact room terms and policy. The hold, confirmation, occupancy, calendar eligibility, account linkage, decision audit and durable reconfirmation job share a transaction. External scheduling follows commit. A changed material term sends the guest back to search. A guest can return to the live visit, request a change, reconfirm or review cancellation. Host email links also reopen settled decisions. See `src/core/booking/decide-stay-request.ts:185`, `src/app/[locale]/requests/visit-actions.ts:20` and `tests/e2e/stay-decision.spec.ts:20`.

The public directory and each listed household accept English and Spanish questions. A returning guest can ask about the hosts’ answer. The conversation and structured form reuse the same availability, signed draft, submission and private status services. Standard forms remain available when browser tools or interpretation are unavailable. See `src/components/guest/guest-conversation.tsx:27`, `src/core/booking/guest-conversation.ts:266` and `tests/e2e/guest-conversation.spec.ts:18`.
